## Full description (max 4000)
RetroCompanion is a companion app for RetroAchievements. See what you've been playing, how close you
are to mastering a game, and get a heads-up when you unlock something new.

What you can do
- Home: your recently played games with progress, the achievements you unlocked in the last 7 days,
  and the current Achievement of the Week.
- Library: every game you've played with completion progress. Pin favorites with a star, and keep a
  wishlist of games you want to play with a bookmark. Import your Want to Play list from the site.
- Search: one search across your games and recent unlocks.
- Game details: every achievement with its badge, points, unlock date and rarity. Filter by unlocked
  or locked, and sort by date, points, rarity or title.
- Stats: your totals, completion by console, the games closest to mastery, your current and longest
  daily unlock streak, and a 26-week activity heatmap.
- Share: turn a completed game or any achievement you've earned into an image card to share.
- Notifications (optional): get notified about new unlocks, with the badge and description. Off by
  default; the check runs about every 15 minutes, so a notification can arrive a little late.
- Works offline with the data it last loaded, and has light and dark themes.

Private by design
Your username and Web API key stay on your device, encrypted. There are no ads, no analytics and no
tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere except to RetroAchievements to fetch your own data.

Getting started
You need a free RetroAchievements account and your own Web API key (retroachievements.org, Settings,
Keys). Enter them in the app's Settings once.

RetroCompanion is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by
RetroAchievements.
